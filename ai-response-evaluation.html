Evaluation framework

How I review technical AI responses

The goal is not only to check whether the final answer looks right, but to inspect the reasoning path that produced it.

Accuracy

Checks whether the final answer and intermediate claims are factually and mathematically correct.

Reasoning quality

Assesses whether the solution follows a valid logical path, states assumptions clearly, and avoids unsupported jumps.

Instruction following

Checks whether the response answers the actual prompt, respects constraints, and uses the requested format.

Clarity and usefulness

Evaluates whether the explanation is understandable, complete, and useful for the intended user.

Sample evaluations

Concrete examples of rubric-based review

Example 1 · Mathematics reasoning evaluation

Combinatorics: handshakes

Prompt

In a room with 12 people, each person shakes hands with every other person exactly once. How many handshakes occur?

Model answer

There are 12 people and each shakes hands with 12 others, so 12 × 12 = 144 handshakes.

Issues found

The model double-counts each handshake and also counts people shaking hands with themselves.

Severity

Major reasoning error.

Corrected answer

Each handshake is a pair of distinct people, so the number is C(12, 2) = 12 × 11 / 2 = 66.

Evaluation rubric

  • Accuracy: incorrect
  • Reasoning: flawed double counting
  • Instruction following: answers the prompt with invalid method
  • Clarity: clear but mathematically wrong

Example 2 · Physics explanation evaluation

Projectile motion: horizontal launch

Prompt

A ball is launched horizontally from a height of 20 m with an initial horizontal speed of 10 m/s. Ignoring air resistance, how long does it take to hit the ground?

Model answer

The time depends on the horizontal speed, so t = d / v. Since the height is 20 m and the speed is 10 m/s, the ball hits the ground in 2 seconds.

Issues found

The model incorrectly uses horizontal speed to compute vertical fall time. Vertical motion is independent of horizontal motion when air resistance is ignored.

Severity

Major physics reasoning error.

Corrected answer

The vertical motion satisfies h = 1/2 g t². With h = 20 m and g ≈ 9.8 m/s², t = sqrt(2h/g) = sqrt(40/9.8) ≈ 2.02 s.

Evaluation rubric

  • Accuracy: numerically close by coincidence
  • Reasoning: incorrect physical model
  • Instruction following: answers the question
  • Clarity: clear but conceptually misleading

Example 3 · Data Science / statistics evaluation

Correlation and causation

Prompt

A dataset shows a strong positive correlation between hours studied and exam scores. Can we conclude that studying more causes higher scores?

Model answer

Yes. Since the correlation is strong and positive, studying more definitely causes higher exam scores.

Issues found

The model confuses correlation with causation and ignores potential confounders, study quality, prior knowledge, selection effects, and experimental design.

Severity

Major conceptual error.

Corrected answer

A strong positive correlation suggests an association, but it does not by itself prove causation. To support a causal claim, one would need an appropriate research design, such as randomized intervention, longitudinal controls, or causal modeling with justified assumptions.

Evaluation rubric

  • Accuracy: incorrect causal conclusion
  • Reasoning: overgeneralizes from correlation
  • Instruction following: answers but overstates certainty
  • Clarity: clear wording with misleading inference

Example 4 · Instruction-following evaluation

Constrained output format

Prompt

Summarize the following paragraph in exactly three bullet points and do not use more than 12 words per bullet.

Model answer

A paragraph-style response with four sentences and no bullet points.

Issues found

The model fails the requested format, number of bullets, and length constraint.

Severity

Moderate to major instruction-following failure.

Corrected answer

Provide exactly three short bullets, each under 12 words.

Evaluation rubric

  • Accuracy: depends on content
  • Reasoning: not central
  • Instruction following: failed
  • Clarity: understandable but violates format
