# Methodological note

This project originated from institutional enrollment records and was redesigned as a public portfolio case.

The public version focuses on the analytical workflow rather than on the original confidential data. It preserves the methodological structure of the work while removing or excluding sensitive inputs.

## Analytical dimensions

1. **Enrollment volume over time**  
   Annual counts of student records by registry year.

2. **Age composition**  
   Age-range distributions and boxplots by year.

3. **Course-level structure**  
   Frequency of entry course by year.

4. **Spatial distribution**  
   Geocoding-based exploration of student residence patterns.

5. **Distance to school**  
   Approximate distance distribution from student residences to the institution.

6. **Sociodemographic aggregation**  
   Aggregate frequencies such as guardian nationality.

## Privacy decisions

The public repository excludes:
- row-level student records;
- original Excel files;
- exact addresses;
- names and surnames;
- Google Drive paths;
- raw geocoding tables;
- screenshots that combine sensitive categories with exact point locations.

The repository may include aggregated screenshots and sanitized methodological notebooks.
